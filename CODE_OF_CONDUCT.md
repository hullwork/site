# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free collaboration environment.

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique designs rather than people, protect private tenant and security data, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or disruptive behavior. Maintainers may edit or reject contributions and may restrict participation when behavior endangers people or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub's reporting mechanisms](https://github.com/convee/site/security/advisories/new) or a repository maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially, and conflicts of interest are avoided where practical.

This policy follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
